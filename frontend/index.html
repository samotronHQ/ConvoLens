about

Hello! I'm Sam — a CSE grad (if that wasn't obvious already).

I like building things that are fun, relatable, and scalable.

ConvoLens started as a small experiment to understand the underlying tones behind conversations. I've always been drawn to patterns, numbers, and the context behind every word — and that's what led me here.

I've also struggled with reading tone in conversations — tone tags helped, but I wanted something that could go a bit deeper.

It's not meant to give you answers or diagnose anything. Think of it more like a catalyst for your own hypotheses. Tip: treat this as a second opinion, not a verdict.

I hope you enjoy using this as much as I enjoyed building it.

You can check out my other (not-so-cool or fun) projects too — github → and let me know what else I should add here.

how it works

01

paste a conversation

Drop in a chat snippet with speakers like A, B, or names. ConvoLens reads who is saying what.

02

the model reads the room

It looks for emotional tone, response effort, imbalance, communication patterns, red flags, and green flags.

03

you get a breakdown

The output is structured into notes so you can see the pattern, evidence, and possible interpretation clearly.

under the hood: FastAPI + Groq/LLaMA + prompt engineering + structured JSON parsing.

built as a GenAI experiment. not therapy, not a verdict — just pattern spotting.
